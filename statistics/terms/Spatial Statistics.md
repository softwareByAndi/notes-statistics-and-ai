#statistics | [[_ Types of Statistics]]
data collected from different locations
-  e.g. census
### geospatial: 
data collected from different locations with spatial coordinates 
- e.g. GPS
### non-geospatial: 
data collected from different locations without spatial coordinates 
- e.g. census
### geostatistical: 
data collected from different locations with spatial coordinates and spatial relationships 
- e.g. GPS
