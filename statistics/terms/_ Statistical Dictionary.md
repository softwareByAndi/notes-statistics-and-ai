#### Explanatory Variables - [[Datum Features]]

the factors or [[Datum Features|features]] you think might cause something to change. 
-  *(aka **independent variables**)*

For example, if you're looking at what affects grades in school, the [[Datum Features|features]] of the dataset could be **grades, study time, attendance, and sleep hours.** 

The explanatory variables would be the **study time, attendance, and sleep hours** features, which would be tested to see if they have an effect on the other features **(grades)**

#### Collinearity - [[Regression]]

is a linear association between two explanatory variables.

#### Multicollinearity - [[Regression]]

extends the concept of collinearity to include **an association among three or more explanatory variables**, where one variable can be linearly predicted from the others with a substantial degree of accuracy.

This leads to redundancy and difficulties in estimating precise effects of independent variables in regression models.

#### Cartesian Product - [[set theory]]

a math term for combining all the possible pairs of items from two sets, showing every combination of items from both sets.
###### example:

$A = \{1,2\}$
$B = \{X,Y\}$
$CartesianProduct(A,B) = \{(1,X), (1,Y), (2,X), (2,Y)\}$
