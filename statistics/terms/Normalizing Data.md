The process of squashing or stretching the data between 0 & 1 so that the min=0 and the max=1.
Normalizing data allows us to compare 2 datasets with different schemas. 
- e.g. Fahrenheit v.s. Celsius
- e.g. comparing price changes between products like cars v.s. fruits v.s. stocks
