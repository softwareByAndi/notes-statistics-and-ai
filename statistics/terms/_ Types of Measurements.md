### definitions

- **spread**: range, variance, standard deviation, etc...  
- **shape**: skewness, kurtosis, etc...
- **association**: correlation, covariance, etc...
- **position**: percentiles, quartiles, etc...
- **dependence**: covariance, correlation, etc...
- **dispersion**: variance, standard deviation, etc...
- **location**: mean, median, mode, etc...
- **shape**: skewness, kurtosis, etc...
- **variability**: range, variance, standard deviation, etc...   

### additional measurements

- **Percentiles** : Value below which a certain percentage of values fall.
- **Quartiles**: Specific Percentiles
    - **e.g.**: 25th (Q1), 50th (Median), and 75th (Q3).
- **Midrange**: Average of the highest and lowest values.
- **Midhinge**: Average of the first (Q1) and third quartiles (Q3).
- **Trimean**: Average of the median and midhinge.
