## definition

the straight-line distance between two points.
#### equation for (n)-dimensional space
$P = (p_1, p_2, ..., p_n)$
$Q = (q_1, q_2, ..., q_n)$
$$
d = \sqrt{(q_1 - p_1)^2 + (q_2 - p_2)^2 + ... + (q_n - p_n)^2}
$$
