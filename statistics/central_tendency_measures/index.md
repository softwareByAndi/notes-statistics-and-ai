## 1. mean

the average of a set of values
- [example code](./mean/mean.md)

**Types**:  
- arithmetic mean
- geometric mean
- harmonic mean
- etc...

**Characteristics**:  
- Affected by extreme values.
- Algebraically treatable (add, subtract, etc.).
- Requires measurement on ALL observations
- Uniquely and comprehensively defined


<br>
<hr>

## 2. median

Middle value of a data set.
- [example code](./median.ipynb)

**Characteristics**:  
- Represents the 50th percentile.
- Not affected by extreme values.
- Cannot be treated algebraically.
- Does NOT require measurement on all observations
- Cannot be uniquely determined under all conditions

<br>
<hr>

## 3. mode

Most frequent value in a data set.
- [example code](./mode.ipynb)

**Characteristics**:  
- Not affected by extreme values.
- Cannot be treated algebraically.
- Does NOT require measurement on all observations
- Not uniquely defined for multi-modal situations. (multiple items w/ same count)
