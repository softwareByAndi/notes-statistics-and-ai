the example below would give a very accurate assessment of fit, but not a good generalization. so note that a perfect fit is not good. we need to model to generalize well, which is usually in contradiction to performing perfectly.

this is typically due to too many variables with too high of polynomials.

the [[bias-variance trade-off]] is a commonly used metric to prevent overfitting.
