#### what does log-odds mean?
Log-odds is the logarithm of the odds ratio. 

In logistic regression, it represents the natural logarithm of the probability that an event occurs divided by the probability that it does not occur.

In logistic regression, the model estimates the log-odds of the dependent variable being 1 (or the event happening) as a linear combination of the independent variables. 

Converting back from log-odds to probability (the metric we're usually interested in) involves the logistic function.
#### what is the LLR p-value?
The LLR p-value is the probability value associated with the [[likelihood ratio test]], which compares the goodness of fit of a model with at least one predictor against a null model with no predictors. 

A low LLR p-value (typically below 0.05) suggests that the model with predictors provides a significantly better fit than the null model.
