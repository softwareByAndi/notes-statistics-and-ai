#linear-algebra

For any symmetric matrix $A$ (or Hermitian matrix in the complex case), there exists an orthogonal matrix $Q$ and a diagonal matrix $D$ such that:
$$
A=QDQ^T
$$

- $A$ is the symmetric matrix or the [[Covariance Matrix]]
- $Q$ is an orthogonal matrix whose columns are [[Eigenvectors]] of $A$
- D is the diagonal matrix whose diagonal elements are the [[Eigenvectors#Eigenvalues |Eigenvalues]] of $A$
